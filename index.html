Fiestang
Pinoy

Kyusi Sunday Sketchers turns one. We're marking it the way we started: a room full of people drawing together.

◷ Sunday, 4 October 2026 ⌖ Racket Room Cubao ◎ 24 slots
94 10th Ave, Cubao, Quezon City

Three themed stations, live models, and small colour groups rotating through the morning. Doors and check-in from 9:00 AM. The official programme runs 9:40 AM–12:40 PM; the venue closes at 1:00 PM.
