Three stations · live models · rotating groups

How It Works

Everyone draws every model, and nobody has to fight for a spot.

Three stations

Amorsolo-ish, Artistic Nude and Encanto. Each has its own model and set-up, and all three run at the same time, so the room is always drawing.

Three groups

Eight people each. You're given a colour tag at check-in, and you stay with that group all morning.

You rotate

After each session your group moves together to its next station. Over the morning every group draws every model.

Inside a station

A short warm-up pose, then one longer pose with proper time to render it — fewer timer resets, more drawing. A station lead keeps time, so you can keep your eyes on the model.

Clean as you go

Every station has a bin bag. Clear your own space between sessions and before you leave, so the next group sits down to a clean station.

Questions we get asked most

FAQ

If yours isn't here, send it our way.

What time should I arrive?

Check-in opens before the programme starts. The exact times go out with the registration form — aim to be signed in and at your first station before the opening, so you don't miss the briefing.

Can I arrive early?

Yes. You're welcome to arrive before the programme begins — just check in with the registration team when you get there and follow their directions while the space is still being set up.

When does the programme start and end?

It's a morning event on Sunday 4 October. We're finalising the venue hours, so the exact start and finish times will be announced with the registration form and confirmed in your registration email.

What are the three stations?

Amorsolo-ish, drawing Filipino light and everyday warmth; Artistic Nude, focused on form, gesture and movement; and Encanto, a Filipino-inspired turn into folklore. Your group rotates through all three.

How does the group rotation work?

You get a colour tag at check-in and that's your group for the morning. Each group starts at a different station and moves together after each session, so everyone draws all three models. Your group's running order is given at the welcome opening.

How long are the sessions?

Each station runs a short warm-up pose and then one longer pose, so you get real time to draw instead of resetting every few minutes. The exact length of the long pose is being finalised, and the station leads keep time on the day.

How many people can join?

24 slots, split into three groups of eight.

What should I bring?

Your own sketching kit — sketchbook or paper, and whatever you like to draw with. Anything else you need to bring goes out with the registration confirmation.

What if I arrive late?

Find the registration team when you arrive and they'll check you in and point you to your group.

Is there parking?

There's no parking at the event space. Street parking around 10th Ave is the only option, so give yourself extra time — or come by transport and save the hunt.

What should I expect at the venue?

Three station areas with a model at each, plus a shared space for the break and the closing activity. Snacks are served during the break. We run clean-as-you-go throughout, so please clear your own space between sessions.

How do I contact the organisers?

Message us on the social channels linked below and one of the organisers will get back to you.
