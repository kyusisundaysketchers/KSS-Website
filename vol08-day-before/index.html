Hi, KSS Sketcher! 👋

Thank you again for joining Kyusi Sunday Sketchers! We're excited to see you tomorrow, Sunday, October 4. 🎨

Here are a few important reminders before the event.

🎨 Event Details
📅 Sunday, October 4, 2026
⏰ Registration opens at 8:30 AM · Program 9:00 AM – 12:00 NN
📍 Racket Room Collective, Cubao, Quezon City
🗺️ OPEN IN MAPS
🗺️ Event Map

After registration, you'll be directed to your assigned group and station. The drawing sessions will rotate between the stations.

⏰ Arrival & Schedule
  • Registration opens at 8:30 AM.
  • The program will begin at 9:00 AM.
  • The program is expected to finish around 12:00 NN. The venue is available until 1:00 PM for packing up.
✏️ What to Bring
🎨 Art Materials
  • Your own drawing materials and a drawing board
  • A chair, if you prefer to have your own

We will provide mats and some chairs in designated areas.

 
☀️ Weather
Parts of the event will take place outdoors, and some activities will be indoors. If it rains, we will move the session indoors and adjust the stations.
  • An umbrella
  • A hat or cap and sunscreen
  • A personal fan or other cooling device
 
🍪 Food & Water
Water and light snacks will be available during the event.
  • A refillable water bottle. Feel free to refill it as needed.
📌 Important
  • This is a registered event, so we cannot accommodate walk-ins. Companions and chaperones also cannot enter.
  • Please keep your belongings with you.
  • Please follow the instructions of the KSS team and facilitators throughout the event.
